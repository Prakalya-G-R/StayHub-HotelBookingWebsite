Zero-Waste Grocery - An Agentic AI Approach to Perishable Demand & Shelf-Life Management

Sustainability

Challenge Statement

Develop an agentic AI proof of concept for selected perishable categories in a simulated grocery network. The system should ingest historical sales, inventory, receipts, prices, promotions, waste, product shelf-life, weather, and local-event data. It should forecast short-term demand, estimate remaining shelf life and spoilage risk, and detect likely stockouts or excess stock. Separate agents may assess demand, freshness, replenishment, markdown, and inter-store transfer options. An orchestration component must compare these options, apply business rules, and propose one action for each affected SKU and store. Every recommendation should state timing, quantity or markdown level, confidence, expected effect, and supporting evidence. A planner must be able to modify, approve, or reject the proposal before action. Use synthetic, public et all and simulated retail-system interfaces.

Background

Perishable grocery categories such as produce, dairy, bakery, and meat have variable shelf lives. Retailers must balance product availability against spoilage while responding to demand, delivery schedules, promotions, weather, events, and customer behavior. Decisions are made across store operations, category management, replenishment, and supply-chain teams using data from point-of-sale, inventory, warehouse, pricing, and waste systems. These sources may be delayed, incomplete, or disconnected, making it difficult to understand remaining shelf life and likely demand for each item at each location. Excess stock leads to markdowns, waste, and lost margin, while insufficient stock causes missed sales and poor customer experience. Existing planning methods may rely on historical averages and fixed rules that cannot respond quickly to local changes. The core need is timely, coordinated decision support that combines demand and freshness information, identifies emerging risks, and helps planners choose replenishment, markdown, or redistribution actions before products expire or shelves become empty.

Deliverables

• Prepared dataset and ingestion workflow for selected sales, inventory, shelf-life, promotion, waste, weather, and event data. • Demand-forecasting and spoilage-risk models for selected SKUs and stores. • Multi-agent recommendation engine with orchestration and configurable business rules. • Planner dashboard for reviewing, modifying, approving, or rejecting proposed actions. • Simulated ERP, WMS, and POS interfaces with decision and feedback logs. • Evaluation report comparing the POC with simple forecasting and rule-based approaches.

Title *

Perishable food waste and inefficient inventory management are significant challenges in grocery retail, where changing customer demand, limited shelf life, and unpredictable market conditions can lead to excess stock, product spoilage, and stockouts. This project proposes FreshGuard AI, an agentic artificial intelligence-based decision-support system designed to optimize perishable inventory management and minimize food waste across a simulated grocery store network.


The system integrates historical sales, inventory, delivery, pricing, promotion, shelf-life, waste, weather, and local-event data to forecast short-term demand and identify potential stock shortages and spoilage risks. It employs specialized AI agents for demand forecasting, freshness assessment, replenishment planning, dynamic markdowns, and inter-store inventory transfers. An orchestration engine evaluates the proposed actions from these agents, applies configurable business rules, and selects appropriate recommendations for each product and store.


Each recommendation provides the proposed action, timing, quantity or markdown level, confidence score, expected impact, and supporting evidence. A planner dashboard enables users to review, modify, approve, or reject recommendations before execution, ensuring human oversight and transparent decision-making. Simulated Point of Sale (POS), Warehouse Management System (WMS), and Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) interfaces support data integration and decision tracking.


The proof of concept will use synthetic, public, and simulated datasets to evaluate demand-forecasting performance, spoilage-risk detection, inventory availability, and potential waste reduction against conventional forecasting and rule-based approaches. FreshGuard AI aims to improve inventory efficiency, reduce avoidable food waste, maintain product availability, and support sustainable grocery retail through coordinated AI agents and human-supervised decision-making.
